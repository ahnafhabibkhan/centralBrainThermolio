WORKSPACE GUIDE

Getting started.

Start with one memory. Review it, approve it, then find it again.

1. Sign in to your workspace.

{% if local %}

On your Mac, open Terminal and paste these two lines. The command copies your local reviewer access key to the clipboard without displaying it.

cd "{{ project_path }}"
.venv/bin/python src/central_brain/manage.py copy-login-key

Return to the sign-in page, paste the key into Local access key with Command+V, and select Open workspace.

Go to sign in → {% else %}

Sign in with your approved Thermolio account and complete the authentication prompt. Contact your workspace administrator if you do not have access.

Sign in{% endif %}

2. Add your first memory.

Choose Add a memory. Select its type, write one clear statement, and identify its source. You can use the example below if it reflects your actual preference.

Type: Preference

Memory: Show project costs in CAD.

Source: My instruction during Central Brain setup.

Select Save proposal. This creates a suggestion for review, not an approved memory.

3. Review and approve it.

Open the proposal in Needs review. Check the wording and source. Select Approve memory to make it available for retrieval, Edit to correct it, or Reject to keep it out of shared context.

Editing an approved memory creates a new proposal. The original stays active until you approve its replacement.

4. Find it again.

Open Search and enter CAD to find the example you approved. Search returns approved, unexpired memories. You can export or delete an individual memory from its details page.

5. Connect your assistants after deployment.

Your regular ChatGPT and Claude apps are not connected through this setup yet. {% endif %}After the approved deployment and connector setup, an assistant can suggest a memory here. You review it on this page, and connected assistants can retrieve it after approval.

Central Brain does not capture every conversation automatically. The Skills page lists reusable procedures only after they have been reviewed and imported.
